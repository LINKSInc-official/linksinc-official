ABOUT LINKS INC.

Shaping minds and the future through academic excellence.

LINKS Inc. brings learners together through national academic tournaments designed to encourage curiosity, confidence, creativity, character, and excellence across different areas of learning.

Through meaningful academic challenges, learners are given opportunities to think deeply, communicate effectively, explore ideas, and celebrate achievement.

Meet LINX, the Knowledge Spark.

LINX is the official LINKS Inc. mascot concept, representing curiosity, knowledge, innovation, and the drive of young knowledge seekers to learn, discover, and excel.
